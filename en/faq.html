FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find the answer? Just drop us a message — we reply quickly.

Will the price stay exactly as quoted?
Yes. We quote a fixed price per route and load size. The total only changes if you add extra cargo or change the address — even a traffic jam won't affect it.
How can I pay?
Cash or bank transfer. You pay after delivery, once you've confirmed everything is in place.
What if I need carrying upstairs and there's no lift?
We'll carry it up for you. The upstairs charge is small and fair: small load +30, medium +50, large +70 zł.
Do you help with loading and unloading?
Yes — loading and unloading are included. It's a van with one driver, and we unload on site ourselves.
Do you do out-of-town trips?
Yes, we cover towns around Wrocław. Out-of-town trips start from 179 zł; beyond 30 km — +3.75 zł/km.
Can I insure my cargo?
Cargo insurance is available — up to the full value of your load. Let us know when booking and we'll talk you through how to arrange it for your move.
What kind of van is it and how much can it carry?
A van with up to 1.4 t payload. Ideal for furniture, appliances, boxes and flat cargo. Not sure if it fits? Send us a message and we'll advise.
